About GEMUN

Where debate becomes diplomacy.

GEMUN is the Model United Nations conference of Gateway Education, Delhi NCR & Sonipat. Delegates step into the shoes of nations, political parties and journalists to tackle the questions shaping our world — from outer space governance to AI-driven warfare.

Beyond the resolutions, GEMUN is about building the skills that last: public speaking, critical research, negotiation and the confidence to lead a room.

Discuss

Rigorous, research-driven debate on real agendas that matter today.

Diplomacy

Build consensus, form blocs and draft solutions that hold up under scrutiny.

Impact

Leave with sharper thinking, a stronger voice and a wider view of the world.
